# APK Installer 0.4.0

Wi-Fi without hunting for it, and testers can remove a third-party app without a terminal.

- Quest 2 / 3 Wi-Fi walkthrough on Connect; one-tap **Connect over Wi-Fi** when an address is saved
- **Switch to Wi-Fi** on Choose apps after USB approval, then unplug
- **Installed apps**: third-party list, search, confirm, remove one at a time (system apps stay hidden)
- Terms and Privacy cover selected-app removal; diagnostics still do not ship a full package inventory
